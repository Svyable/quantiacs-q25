What is new

Six sponsor-provided on-chain series supply 21 features: transaction counts, active addresses, estimated transaction volume, hash rate, mining difficulty and miner revenue, plus activity and mining ratios. Trailing medians and bounded rolling normalization temper spikes. The prior strategy signal banks are unused. MVRV and NVT were excluded because their sponsor histories stop in October 2022.

Four ridge models combine 365- and 1,095-day training windows with 7- and 21-day outcomes. They refit each Monday, exponentially favor recent samples, and train only on outcomes completed before the decision. Their mean forecast and disagreement set long-only exposure. A separate inverse-volatility basket uses historical liquidity, a 25% name cap and a 30% estimated annual risk target. The target is not a realized risk guarantee; rebalancing occurs Monday and Thursday.

On-chain observations are delayed three calendar days, with a seven-day stress test. Historical release and revision vintages are unverified, so a delayed snapshot cannot by itself prove point-in-time availability.

What the backtests teach us

The development advantage over mismatched labels is just 0.0145 Sharpe. Since 2023 the primary improves on the price-only and mismatched-label controls but underperforms simply holding the risk-managed basket. Recently even the zero-slippage primary loses: Sharpe -0.45. This is not explained solely by modeled trading costs. Broad-market exposure timing is the failed component of this tested design.

What was verified

Official Quantiacs statistics match the reported full Sharpe to numerical precision. Cleaner changes are zero. Weights are finite, nonnegative, at most 25% per asset and at most 100% gross; the official liquidity check found zero nonliquid dates. Prefix and 1,825-day bounded-history recomputations match exactly at three dates: 2017-11-16, 2022-06-13 and 2026-09-14. These are checkpoint tests, not an exhaustive daily multipass run.

Local daily-return correlation with CRYPTO10 is 0.622 over the full sample. This is not the hosted competitor-correlation test. No competition submission or correlation-service upload was performed.

Competition Sharpe: not established

All history through 2026-09-15 was already exposed during prior research. Later windows are reused diagnostics, not an untouched final holdout. The 2,000-resample, 30-day paired circular block bootstrap gives a conditional since-2023 Sharpe interval of [-0.49, 1.87]. It includes zero, does not correct for the full history of strategy selection, and is not a forecast interval for the next contest. No defensible positive prospective Sharpe has been established.

Next research checkpoint

  1. Retire this exposure-timing hypothesis. Preserve this record instead of extending its parameter search.
  2. Test a new information channel. Sponsor futures prices could support a cross-market model, but both futures data requests returned HTTP 500 here. Metadata alone was available; this hypothesis remains untested.
  3. Separate selection from market direction. A future relative-return study should first measure rank information and incremental return against exposure-matched controls. It needs a distinct new signal and its own frozen protocol.
  4. Keep future observations unexamined for selection. Record predictions from a frozen version after the cutoff; prospective data is needed before treating historical success as a competition estimate.
